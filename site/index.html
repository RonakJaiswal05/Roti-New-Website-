How it works

From our kitchen to a child's plate.

Roti Foundation Mumbai began by collecting surplus food from events. Since January 2020 we cook every meal ourselves, so we control what goes on a child's plate and when it arrives.

Cooked fresh

In our own kitchen, to one food-safety standard.

Carried in our vans

Our own fleet takes meals to school gates and hospitals.

Served in class

18,000+ children in 53 schools, every school day.

Counted for you

Every meal is counted in the monthly Samachar, and in your receipt.

Our founder

A life of public service, now devoted to feeding children.

Shri D. Sivanandhan, IPS (Retd.), retired as Director General of Police, Maharashtra, in 2011, after commanding the police of Mumbai, Thane and Nagpur. In December 2017 he founded Roti Foundation Mumbai to make sure the city's hungry, and above all its children, get a proper meal.
