Our work

Five programmes. One kitchen standard.

Since January 2020, every meal is cooked in our own kitchen and carried in our own vans, so we control what goes on each plate and when it arrives.

School Nutrition Programme

Fresh meals and fortified snacks for children in municipal schools. Regular, nutritious food improves their health, their attendance and their learning, and nurtures not only their bodies but their dreams.

Hospital meals

Patients and their families often travel long distances for treatment and wait for days outside hospitals and cancer centres. Roti Foundation Mumbai serves them a freshly cooked meal every day, so they can focus on recovery.

1,000+patients and families a day
2hospitals

How you can help: give, volunteer at the gate, or sponsor a meal for a special occasion.

Volunteer at a hospital

Kitchen and rations

Housing societies, schools and companies collect dry rations, which our kitchen turns into meals. Donors can visit and see the journey from raw ingredients to the final meal. Families also mark birthdays, anniversaries and memories with a meal for children.

8,940 kgfrom Konkan Railway in one gift, Dec 2025

How you can help: run a ration drive, or dedicate meals to an occasion.

Start a ration drive

Community events

National days, World Food Day, festivals and Teachers' Day are marked with sweets and snacks for schoolchildren, and with community service such as beach clean-ups. Every celebration is shared with the children we serve.

How you can help: sponsor an event, or bring your team for a volunteering day.
