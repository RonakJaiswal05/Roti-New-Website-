An honest note on "live"

Which numbers are live, and which are not.

No official source publishes India's hunger or food-waste figures in real time. The national surveys are yearly or periodic. The two food-waste counters above are transparent estimates: they spread the official yearly or daily figure evenly over time, and each one says so.

The truly live numbers are Roti Foundation Mumbai's own, from the kitchen, the vans and the volunteers. Being this clear is what lets journalists and CSR teams quote this page with confidence.

For CSR teams: this work maps to SDG 2 (Zero Hunger) and SDG 12.3 (halving food waste), and to Schedule VII, Item (i) of the Companies Act.
