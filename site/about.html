About Roti Foundation Mumbai

No child in our city should study hungry.

Roti Foundation Mumbai believes food is a basic human right. We work towards a healthier Bharat where every child has wholesome food, so hunger never holds back what they can become.

Our story

From surplus food to our own kitchen.

Roti Foundation Mumbai began by collecting excess food from events and carrying it to people who needed it. In January 2020 we opened our own kitchen, so every meal is freshly cooked to one standard. Today we serve the most deprived areas of the Mumbai Metropolitan Region, and six more cities.

Founder & Managing Trustee

Founder

Shri D. Sivanandhan, IPS (Retd.)

Shri Sivanandhan retired as Director General of Police, Maharashtra, in March 2011, after serving as Commissioner of Police for Mumbai, Thane and Nagpur, and six years in the Mumbai Crime Branch, where he is known for significantly weakening the underworld at its peak. He also served 11 years with the Intelligence Bureau and the Central Bureau of Investigation.

He has taught at the University of Madras and co-authored the national bestseller Chanakya's Seven Secrets of Leadership. His honours include the President's Distinguished Service Medal (2000), the Meritorious Service Medal (1993) and the Internal Security Medal (1998).
