Is my donation tax deductible?

Yes. Roti Foundation Mumbai is registered under Section 80G. Add your PAN at checkout and your receipt arrives on WhatsApp and email within minutes. Each year we also file Form 10BD and send you your Form 10BE certificate before 31 May.

Why ₹40?

₹40 is what it costs Roti Bank to cook and deliver one fresh, nutritious meal for a child, as published in the Samachar. Your amount is shown in meals so you always know what it became.

Can I pause or cancel a monthly gift?

Any time, in one tap, from My account. You'll never need to call or email us to stop a mandate.

Can I give from outside India?

Roti Bank can accept gifts from Indian bank accounts and cards. For gifts from abroad, write to us and we'll guide you.

Can my company match my gift?

Yes. See CSR partnerships for payroll giving and employee volunteering days.
