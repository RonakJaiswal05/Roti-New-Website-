About Roti Foundation Mumbai · 100 words

Roti Foundation Mumbai (Roti Foundation Mumbai) is a public charitable trust founded in December 2017 by Shri D. Sivanandhan, IPS (Retd.), former Director General of Police, Maharashtra. It began by collecting surplus food from events, and since January 2020 has cooked every meal in its own kitchen. Roti Foundation Mumbai feeds 18,000+ children in 53 Mumbai municipal schools every school day, serves patients' families outside hospitals, and runs community meals in 7 cities across five states. It has served 31,192,568 meals to Oct 2026. One meal for a child costs ₹40. Roti Foundation Mumbai is 80G and CSR-1 registered.
