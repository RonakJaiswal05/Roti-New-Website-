About Roti Bank · 100 words

Mumbai Roti Bank (Roti Foundation Mumbai) is a public charitable trust founded in December 2017 by Shri D. Sivanandhan, IPS (Retd.), former Director General of Police, Maharashtra. It began by collecting surplus food from events, and since January 2020 has cooked every meal in its own kitchen. Roti Bank feeds {{fact:children_daily}} children in {{fact:schools}} Mumbai municipal schools every school day, serves patients' families outside hospitals, and runs community meals in {{fact:cities}} cities across five states. It has served {{fact:meals_served}} meals to {{fact:meals_served|as_of}}. One meal for a child costs {{fact:meal_cost}}. Roti Bank is 80G and CSR-1 registered.
