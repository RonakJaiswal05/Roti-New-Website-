Our founder

A lifetime in the police. Then a new mission: hunger.

Shri D. Sivanandhan, IPS (Retd.), retired as Director General of Police, Maharashtra, in 2011, after commanding the police of Mumbai, Thane and Nagpur. In December 2017 he founded Mumbai Roti Bank to make sure the city's hungry, and above all its children, get a proper meal.
