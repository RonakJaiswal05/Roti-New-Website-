Ration Drive Kit

Start a drive in two minutes.

Collect dry rations in your building, school or office. You get a drive page and a WhatsApp poster. When it closes, book a pickup: our kitchen weighs in every kilogram and sends you a certificate in kilograms and meals.

This month we need: rice, tur dal, wheat flour, cooking oil, jaggery and poha. No cooked food, please.

Internships

Learn how a food programme really runs.

Interns work on distribution, outreach, social media, data and fundraising, alongside the Roti Foundation Mumbai team. Applications move from applied to interview to onboarded, and your letter is generated from your record when you finish.

Kitchen visits

See your rations become meals.

Donors and drive organisers can visit our kitchen and watch the morning's cooking, from raw rations to packed meals. Visits are in small groups, on weekday mornings.
